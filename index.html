Hexathlon: an arcade athletics game

Mash two keys in turn to build speed, then time your jumps and throws across six Events played in a fixed order: 100m Dash, Long Jump, Javelin Throw, 110m Hurdles, Hammer Throw and High Jump. Reach the Standard in every Event to start the next Cycle, where the Standards get tougher. Play with a keyboard, a gamepad or a touch screen; the game installs as an app and plays offline.

Hexathlon needs a browser with canvas support.
